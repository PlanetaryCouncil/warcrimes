P1HonestReporting: an Israel advocacy group, in its own words

Founded October 2000 · About page read 26 Oct 2026
  • Its About page says “Israel” 12 times in 545 words. “Palestinian”: zero.
  • Accused Gaza photographers of foreknowledge of 7 October, watched politicians call for them to be “eliminated,” then admitted it had no evidence.
  • Mission: fairness in journalism “as it impacts Israel.” Also on offer: “the top choice in luxury trips” to Israel.

HonestReporting presents itself as a media watchdog, but its own mission statement limits its concern for fairness in journalism to coverage "as it impacts Israel"; it was founded in October 2000 by Shaul Rosenblatt of Aish HaTorah UK, has been led since 2022 by former AIPAC regional director Jacki Alexander, and critics including Reporters Without Borders and the Canadian Association of Journalists have described its campaigns against reporters as intimidation. In November 2023 it insinuated that Gaza photojournalists working for AP, Reuters, CNN and The New York Times had advance knowledge of the 7 October attack; the outlets showed their photos were taken after it began, Israeli politicians called for the photographers to be targeted, and HonestReporting's executive director then conceded the group had no evidence and had only "raised questions."

So honest about its bias that it put it in the first paragraph.
⚠ HonestReporting says it is not affiliated with any government or party; per Wikipedia, in 2024 it took part in a strategy session with Foreign Minister Gideon Sa'ar on a $150 million state “public diplomacy” initiative. Word counts are of the About page's main text (mission, principles and strategy) as read on 26 Oct 2026.
