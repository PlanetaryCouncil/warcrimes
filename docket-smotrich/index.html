D4Bezalel Smotrich — Finance Minister

Huwara remark: 1 Mar 2023; starvation remark: 5 Aug 2024; five-country sanctions: 10 Jun 2025
  • “The village of Huwara needs to be wiped out.”
  • Said starving two million Gazans might be “justified and moral.”
  • Sanctioned by the UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand and Norway.

Smotrich said in March 2023 that "the village of Huwara needs to be wiped out" and that "the State of Israel should do it" (Times of Israel, Al Jazeera), and in August 2024 told the Katif Conference that "nobody will let us cause two million civilians to die of hunger even though it might be justified and moral until our hostages are returned" (CNN), a remark the UN High Commissioner for Human Rights condemned as justifying the war crime of starving civilians. On 10 June 2025, the UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand and Norway imposed sanctions on him — asset freezes and travel bans — for having "incited extremist violence" against Palestinians in the West Bank, per the joint foreign ministers' statement.
