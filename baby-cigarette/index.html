06Gaza toddler returned from Israeli detention with reported cigarette burns

19 Mar 2026
  • A 21-month-old returned from detention with circular burns doctors said match cigarettes.
  • Ten hours in military custody; his father did not come back with him.
  • X-rays ruled out the army's shrapnel explanation, Gaza doctors said.

On 19 March 2026, Israeli forces detained 21-month-old Jawad Abu Nassar and his father Osama near the al-Maghazi refugee camp in central Gaza, and the toddler was handed back through the Red Cross about ten hours later with circular wounds on his legs that his family and doctors at a Gaza hospital said were consistent with deliberate cigarette burns. The Israeli military rejected the abuse allegations as unfounded, suggesting the marks were splinter or shrapnel injuries, while the father reportedly remained in detention.
