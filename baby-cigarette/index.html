06Gaza toddler returned from Israeli detention with reported cigarette burns

19 Mar 2026
  • A 21-month-old returned from detention with circular burns doctors said match cigarettes.
  • Ten hours in military custody; his father never came back — and the only source for his “Hamas confession” is his interrogators.
  • X-rays ruled out the army's shrapnel explanation, Gaza doctors said.

On 19 March 2026, Israeli forces detained 21-month-old Jawad Abu Nassar and his father Osama near the al-Maghazi refugee camp in central Gaza, and the toddler was handed back through the Red Cross about ten hours later with circular wounds on his legs that his family and doctors at a Gaza hospital said were consistent with deliberate cigarette burns. The Israeli military rejected the abuse allegations as unfounded, suggesting the marks were splinter or shrapnel injuries, while the father reportedly remained in detention.

⚠ The IDF says that “during questioning, he identified himself as a Hamas operative,” admitted entering Israel on 7 October, and “confessed to bringing his toddler son” as a human shield. The father has not confirmed any of it: he has been held incommunicado since 19 March 2026, and every account of his words traces to the army that detained him, which is also accused of abusing his son. His family rejects the claim (“why would my son carry his infant on his shoulder and approach the Yellow Line if he had any party affiliations?”) and describes a mental breakdown after he lost his livelihood; the ICRC told the family he had been shot in the shoulder, while the IDF says it fired warning shots. Drop Site News names the family al-Shafi rather than Abu Nassar. HonestReporting's rebuttal (marked as propaganda) cites no independent forensic exam.
