D10Giora Eiland — Retired Maj. Gen., architect of the "Generals' Plan"

Yedioth Ahronoth "epidemics" op-ed: 19 Nov 2023; cited in South Africa's ICJ application: 29 Dec 2023; "Generals' Plan" published/debated: Sep–Oct 2024; Haaretz soldiers' testimony and north Gaza siege reporting: Oct 2024
  • The “Generals' Plan”: seal northern Gaza, give a week to leave, treat whoever stays as a combatant.
  • Wrote that epidemics in Gaza would “bring victory closer.”
  • Reporting from late 2024 suggests the plan stopped being hypothetical.

Eiland, a retired IDF major general and former National Security Council chief, authored the October 2024 "Generals' Plan" to give northern Gaza's roughly 300,000 residents one week to leave before the area was sealed under "full and tight blockade" — cutting food, water and fuel so those remaining, treated as combatants, would "either have to surrender or to starve" — and in a November 2023 Yedioth Ahronoth op-ed wrote that "epidemics in the south [of Gaza] will bring victory closer and will decrease casualties among IDF soldiers," a statement 15 Israeli public-health experts condemned as advocating the weaponization of disease. His statements were cited in South Africa's 29 December 2023 ICJ genocide application as evidence of genocidal intent, and although the plan was never officially adopted, Israeli reserve soldiers told Haaretz in October 2024 that "the Eiland Plan is being promoted by the IDF" during the siege of Jabalia and north Gaza — while no court has brought proceedings against Eiland personally.

🇮🇱 Official responseEiland and the IDF deny the plan is being implemented (TIME, Nov 2024; Eiland also said on 10 Dec 2024 the plan was "not being implemented at all")
