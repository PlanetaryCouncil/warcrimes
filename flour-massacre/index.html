12Al-Rashid Street aid convoy killings, Gaza City

29 Feb 2024
  • Over 100 killed and some 750 wounded around a flour convoy — people starving in line.
  • CNN's forensic review found gunfire began before the army's own timeline says the crossing started.
  • Israel said victims were trampled; the hospitals counted gunshot wounds.

On 29 February 2024, at least 112 Palestinians were killed and roughly 760 wounded around an aid-flour convoy at the Nabulsi roundabout on al-Rashid Street in Gaza City; witnesses and Gaza health officials said Israeli forces fired on the crowd, while the Israeli military said most victims were trampled or run over in a crush around the trucks. UN experts condemned the killings and called for accountability, and a CNN review of video evidence and witness testimony, assessed by forensic experts, cast doubt on the Israeli military's timeline, indicating gunfire began before the stated start of the convoy crossing.
