07BAttack dogs: torture and reported rape of Palestinian detainees

Accounts published June 2024 – November 2026; export records October 2023 – February 2025 · Last updated 9 Nov 2026
  • Reported rape involving dogs: Euro-Med published a witness account in 2024 and a separate survivor account in 2026.
  • Dog attacks and intimidation are reported in detention facilities and during raids in Gaza and the West Bank.
  • SOMO obtained 110 veterinary export certificates issued to Dutch dog companies for exports to Israel; 100 were for Four Winds K9.

Euro-Med Human Rights Monitor published a witness account in June 2024 and a survivor account in April 2026 alleging that Israeli forces used dogs to rape Palestinian detainees. Its reporting also describes dogs used to attack and intimidate people during detention and raids. A 5 November 2026 investigation by Rajaa Natour for The Electronic Intifada examines the European supply chain behind military dogs; SOMO’s research documents Dutch veterinary export certificates and the absence of military-style licensing controls for these exports.

Reported rape involving dogs — two published accounts

June 2024 — witness account. Euro-Med published testimony from lawyer Fadi Saif al-Din Bakr, who said he saw soldiers restrain another detainee and use dogs to rape him. Read Euro-Med’s account and attribution →

April 2026 — survivor account. Euro-Med published testimony from Wajdi, 43, who alleged that he was raped by soldiers and a dog during interrogation. Read the later report →

These are published testimonies recorded by Euro-Med, not judicial findings. Repetition of the same account in later articles is not an additional incident. The accounts do not establish a total number of victims or locate every incident at Sde Teiman.

New reporting — 5 November 2026

Exported to Israel as pets, Dutch dogs used to torture Palestinians — Rajaa Natour, The Electronic Intifada.

The article follows the Dutch supply chain and interviews researchers about export oversight, corporate responsibility and the reported use of dogs against Palestinians. Amended 6 November 2026.

The Dutch supply chain — what the records show

SOMO’s April 2025 investigation obtained 110 veterinary certificates issued between October 2023 and February 2025 to Dutch military/police dog companies for exports to Israel: 100 to Four Winds K9, four to Police Dogs Centre Holland and six to K10 Working Dogs.

Certificates document exports; they do not identify every end user, demonstrate that every dog went to the military, or link a particular exported dog to an alleged assault. ARIJ’s investigation examines the wider European trade →

Scope and official response

This focus brings together allegations from detention and raids across different locations. The general IDF statement on Sde Teiman is indexed in 07 — torture and sexual violence, alongside the broader detention record and PHRI’s custody-death investigation. That statement is not a specific response to each dog-related allegation.
