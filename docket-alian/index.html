D8Ghassan Alian — Maj. Gen., head of COGAT

Statement: 10 Oct 2023 (video; parallel X post 9 Oct); cited in South Africa's ICJ application filed 29 Dec 2023; HRW report: 19 Dec 2024; Hind Rajab Foundation ICC/Italy arrest request: 13 Jan 2025
  • “Human animals must be treated as such… You wanted hell, you will get hell.”
  • He ran COGAT — the agency controlling every calorie entering Gaza.
  • His words appear in South Africa's genocide application at the ICJ.

In a 10 October 2023 video address to Gaza residents reported by The Times of Israel, Maj. Gen. Ghassan Alian — head of COGAT, the Israeli military body that controls the entry of aid into Gaza — declared: "Human animals must be treated as such. There will be no electricity and no water, there will only be destruction. You wanted hell, you will get hell." South Africa cited the statement as evidence of genocidal intent in its ICJ Genocide Convention case against Israel; Human Rights Watch's December 2024 report "Extermination and Acts of Genocide" documented COGAT's blocking of water, fuel, and aid equipment under his command; and in January 2025 the Hind Rajab Foundation filed requests with the ICC and Italian authorities seeking his arrest for genocide, crimes against humanity, and war crimes.

🇮🇱 Official responseIsrael's pleadings in ICJ Case 192 deny the genocide allegations; on the Jan 2025 arrest request, COGAT declined to comment (per The Times of Israel)
