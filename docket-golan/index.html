D6May Golan — Minister for Social Equality

“Proud of the ruins of Gaza”: 19 Feb 2024 (Knesset) · “another Nakba”: Oct 2024 · police recommend indictment (corruption, unrelated): 10 Jun 2026 · Last updated 10 Nov 2026
  • “I am proud of the ruins of Gaza!” — her own words, posted on X with the Knesset video the night she said them.
  • October 2024, at a rally for settling Gaza: “another Nakba,” per NPR.
  • No Gaza charges. The trial police recommended in June 2026 is about jobs and money at home.

Speaking in the Knesset on 19 February 2024, during the vote to expel MK Ofer Cassif over his support for South Africa's genocide case, Minister May Golan said she was “proud of the ruins of Gaza,” that “every baby, even 80 years from now, will tell their grandchildren what the Jews did” after October 7, and that there should be “no dove and no olive leaf, only a sword — to cut off Sinwar's head”; she posted the speech and the words on X the same evening. In October 2024, NPR reported, she told a rally for Jewish settlement in Gaza that there should be “another Nakba.” No court has charged her over Gaza; in June 2026 Israeli police recommended she stand trial in a separate corruption case.

The poster says the world will forget her. Maybe not: Israeli police have already recommended she stand trial — just not for this.
